About the Museum

Science made accessible for everyone through discovery, learning, and community.

Our Mission

The Community Science Museum creates a space where everyone can experience the wonders of discovery and explore the world around them.

Science should not be confined to textbooks. Through interactive exhibits, visitors can see science come to life.

Get Involved
